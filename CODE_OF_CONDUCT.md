# PrePaste Code of Conduct

Hey, welcome to PrePaste! ❤️

PrePaste is an open-source project, and I want it to be a place where anyone feels comfortable contributing, whether you're a beginner or have been coding for years.

There aren't a ton of rules here, just a few things to keep the community friendly and enjoyable for everyone.

## Be Nice to Each Other

- **Be respectful.** Different opinions are welcome, but please be considerate of others.
- **Be patient.** We all start somewhere. No question is too small or silly.
- **Give constructive feedback.** It's okay to point out issues, just be thoughtful about how you do it.
- **Be welcoming.** Whether someone's fixing a typo or submitting a big feature, their contribution matters.
- **No harassment or hate.** Bullying, discrimination, personal attacks, or intentionally making others uncomfortable won't be tolerated.

## It's Okay to Disagree

Not everyone will agree on every feature, design choice, or piece of code, and that's perfectly normal.

Just keep conversations about the project rather than making things personal. A little kindness goes a long way.

## Reporting an Issue

If someone is making you or others uncomfortable, please contact the maintainer privately.

I'll do my best to handle concerns fairly. Depending on the situation, this may mean removing comments, closing discussions, or restricting someone from participating.

## Keep Communication Clear and Professional

When opening an issue, submitting a pull request, or joining a discussion, try to keep things clear, simple, and to the point.

A few things to keep in mind:

- **Keep it clear.** Explain what you're changing, suggesting, or reporting without making things unnecessarily complicated.
- **Keep it professional.** Try to avoid emojis in pull requests, issues, and technical discussions. They're fine in casual conversations, but technical contributions are better kept clean and focused.
- **Be yourself.** You don't need fancy words, lengthy explanations, or perfectly polished writing. Just explain things in your own words.


**Good work speaks for itself.**


## A Little Reminder ♡

You don't need to be an expert to contribute to PrePaste.

Bug reports, suggestions, questions, documentation fixes, and tiny improvements are all appreciated.

At the end of the day, this is just a little project we're building together, and I'd love for it to be a nice place to do that.

Thanks for being here, and happy contributing! ❤️
